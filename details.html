About Priyadarshini Junior College

Priyadarshini Junior College is a leading intermediate institution located in Nellore, dedicated to academic excellence and holistic student development.

Vision

To empower young minds with knowledge, discipline, and ethical values.

Mission

To deliver high-quality education that promotes critical thinking, creativity, and leadership.

Courses Offered

  • PCMB - Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology
  • PCMC - Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science
  • CEBA - Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy
  • SEBA - Statistics, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy
  • HEBA - History, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy
  • HESP - History, Economics, Sociology, Political Science

Facilities

  • Smart classrooms
  • Science and computer laboratories
  • Library with e-resources
  • Sports and cultural programs
  • Bus and hostel facilities

Achievements

Students have excelled in board exams, sports, and cultural competitions at state and national levels.
